Get ready
👕 Fitted clothing — fitted t-shirt with trousers or shorts. Avoid baggy tops, hoodies and open shirts — they inflate measurements.
🎨 Contrast with the wall — stand against a plain wall and make sure your clothing clearly stands out from it. All-black clothing on any wall and light clothing on a light wall both reduce accuracy. A mid-colour top on a plain white or light-grey wall works best.
🦾 Arms slightly away — hold arms a few centimetres from your sides, palms facing in. Don't press them against your body and don't spread them wide — both cause measurement errors.
📏 Full body visible — stand 2–3 m from the camera. Head to toe must be in frame. Stand straight, feet together, facing forward.
💡 Even lighting — bright, even light on the front of your body. Avoid shadows across the body or strong light from behind.
